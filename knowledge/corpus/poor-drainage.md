## Symptoms

Water sits on top of the soil for a long time after watering, or pools in the
saucer and never fully drains. The medium stays soggy days after watering even
though the plant has not been overwatered by volume. Leaves yellow and the plant
looks generally unhappy without a clear trigger.

## Where on the plant symptoms appear

No consistent pattern on the plant itself at first; symptoms are visible mainly
in the soil and pot. If left uncorrected, whole-plant yellowing and root rot follow
because roots sit in stagnant water.

## Look-alikes and how to tell them apart

Overwatering looks identical but is caused by watering too often, not by the pot's
inability to drain; reducing watering frequency alone resolves overwatering but not
poor drainage. Root rot is the downstream consequence once drainage failure has
persisted for weeks — check whether the pot itself has no holes or is sitting in a
sealed decorative cachepot before assuming rot has already set in.

## Confirming test the user can perform

Water the plant and time how long it takes for water to appear at the drainage
hole. If nothing emerges within a minute or two, or the saucer fills and stays
full, check whether the pot has holes at all and whether a decorative outer pot is
trapping water underneath.

## Treatment, least-invasive first

Empty the saucer immediately and elevate the pot on pot feet or gravel so it never
sits in standing water. Check that drainage holes are not blocked by compacted
roots or debris and clear them. If the pot has no holes, repot into one that does.
If the medium itself has broken down into a dense, water-retentive mass, repot into
a fresh, coarser mix with perlite or bark added.

## Expected time to visible improvement

Drainage improves immediately once water can escape. Plant vigour recovers over
two to four weeks as the root zone dries between waterings.

## Prognosis and when to give up

Very good once the mechanical cause is fixed, provided the roots have not already
rotted. If rot has set in, treat as root rot in addition to fixing drainage.
