## Symptoms

Water-soaked spots appear on leaves and darken to brown or black, often with a
yellow halo around the edge. Spots tend to be angular or irregular, bounded by
leaf veins, and can look greasy or wet. In humid weather they may ooze slightly
when disturbed.

## Where on the plant symptoms appear

Scattered across leaves of any age, spreading fastest where water splashes from
leaf to leaf, so lower and outer leaves exposed to splash are often hit hardest.

## Look-alikes and how to tell them apart

Fungal leaf spot produces well-defined, roughly circular spots with a dry,
papery centre and sometimes concentric rings, unlike bacterial spot's angular,
water-soaked, vein-bounded shape. Anthracnose forms sunken, dry lesions
concentrated at leaf tips and margins rather than scattered water-soaked spots.
Chemical damage causes irregular patches too, but tied to a specific contact
event rather than the halo and greasy look bacterial spot has.

## Confirming test the user can perform

Hold an affected leaf up to the light: a water-soaked, slightly translucent halo
around a dark spot suggests bacterial infection. Gently squeeze a fresh spot
between two fingers in humid conditions; a sticky, cloudy ooze supports a
bacterial cause, which a dry fungal spot will not produce.

## Treatment, least-invasive first

Avoid watering onto leaves and stop misting, since bacteria spread readily by
water splash. Remove and discard affected leaves, and disinfect cutting tools
between cuts. Improve airflow to help leaf surfaces dry quickly after any
wetting. Space the plant away from others while active. Copper-based products
can slow spread as a last resort; follow the product label exactly, since there
is no cure once tissue is infected.

## Expected time to visible improvement

No new spots within one to two weeks of stopping overhead watering and removing
affected leaves.

## Prognosis and when to give up

Fair. Bacterial infections are harder to stop than fungal ones since no
fungicide treats them; persistent spread despite dry conditions is a poor sign.
