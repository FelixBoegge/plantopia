## Symptoms

Small, dark, mosquito-like flies hover around the soil surface and scatter when
the plant is disturbed or watered. Adults are mostly a nuisance, but their larvae
live in the top layer of soil and can damage fine roots or seedlings when
numbers are high, causing slowed growth or wilting in severe cases.

## Where on the plant symptoms appear

Activity is concentrated at the soil surface rather than on the leaves. Root
damage, when it happens, affects fine roots near the surface, which can show up
as generalized wilting or stunted growth.

## Look-alikes and how to tell them apart

Whitefly also flies up when disturbed, but whitefly are found on and fly from
the leaves themselves, not the soil surface. Overwatering shares the same damp
soil conditions that attract fungus gnats but does not by itself produce flying
insects. If wilting is the main complaint, check the roots as for root rot before
assuming gnat larvae are the cause, since heavy larval damage is uncommon except
in constantly wet soil.

## Confirming test the user can perform

Water the plant and watch the soil surface for a minute. Small dark flies
rising up and hovering confirm fungus gnats. Pushing a finger into the top
centimetre of soil that stays constantly damp explains why they are established.

## Treatment, least-invasive first

Let the soil surface dry out more between waterings, since larvae need
consistently moist soil to survive. Remove any standing water in a saucer. Cover
the soil surface with a thin layer of sand or fine gravel, which discourages
egg-laying. Yellow sticky traps placed near the soil catch adults and help
monitor numbers. Only if the infestation persists, apply a biological soil
drench containing beneficial nematodes or Bacillus thuringiensis israelensis,
following the product label exactly.

## Expected time to visible improvement

Adult numbers should drop noticeably within one to two weeks of letting the soil
dry out more between waterings.

## Prognosis and when to give up

Excellent. Fungus gnats are mostly a watering-habit problem and resolve reliably
once the soil surface is allowed to dry.
