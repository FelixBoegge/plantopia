## Symptoms

A scorched-looking brown band develops along the tips and margins of leaves while
the leaf centre stays green. Small brown spots sometimes appear just inside the
margin. Affected leaves may curl or feel slightly crisp at the edge. The plant
otherwise looks healthy in the centre of each leaf.

## Where on the plant symptoms appear

The oldest leaves first, since potassium is mobile and is moved to newer growth
when supply is short. The scorch band spreads inward from the margin over weeks
rather than appearing suddenly.

## Look-alikes and how to tell them apart

Fertiliser burn also scorches margins but appears suddenly within days of feeding
and affects leaves of all ages at once, often with a visible salt crust on the
soil; potassium deficiency develops slowly on old leaves only, unconnected to a
recent feeding. Low humidity crisps tips too, but as a dry, even browning rather
than a distinct band with spotting just inside it. Magnesium deficiency produces
blotchy yellowing between the veins rather than a solid marginal band.

## Confirming test the user can perform

Examine the two or three oldest leaves. If a continuous brown band runs around the
outer edge while the centre and veins stay green, no fertiliser was applied in the
last two weeks, and there is no white crust on the soil, potassium deficiency is
likely.

## Treatment, least-invasive first

Refresh old, depleted potting mix at the next repot rather than relying on old
soil to keep supplying nutrients. Work in wood ash or compost sparingly to raise
potassium levels gradually. If scorch continues to spread, apply a fertiliser
with a meaningful potassium ratio, following the product label's rate and
frequency exactly.

## Expected time to visible improvement

New leaves should emerge without the marginal scorch within four to six weeks of
correcting feeding. Existing scorched tissue does not recover.

## Prognosis and when to give up

Good with consistent feeding. If scorch keeps appearing on new leaves despite
correct fertilising, check for salt buildup or root damage restricting uptake.
