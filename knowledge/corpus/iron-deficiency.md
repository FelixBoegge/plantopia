## Symptoms

The newest, smallest leaves at the growing tip turn pale yellow to almost white
between the veins, while the veins themselves stay distinctly green, giving a
net-like pattern. In severe cases the whole young leaf can bleach nearly white.
Growth at the tip slows.

## Where on the plant symptoms appear

The newest growth first, because iron is immobile and the plant cannot move it
from older leaves to supply new ones. Older, lower leaves stay fully green even
as new leaves bleach.

## Look-alikes and how to tell them apart

Magnesium deficiency produces the identical interveinal, net-like pattern but on
the oldest leaves rather than the newest, because magnesium is mobile and iron is
not. Nitrogen deficiency yellows old leaves uniformly, veins included, rather than
sparing the veins on new growth. High soil pH is a common underlying cause of true
iron deficiency and is treated the same way, so distinguishing them further is not
necessary for a home diagnosis.

## Confirming test the user can perform

Look at the smallest, newest leaves at the growing tip. If the tissue between the
veins is pale yellow or white while the veins stand out distinctly green, and
leaves lower down the stem remain fully green, iron deficiency is likely.

## Treatment, least-invasive first

Check the water source and soil pH indirectly by considering whether hard tap
water or an alkaline potting mix has been used, since high pH locks iron out of
reach even when present. Switch to rainwater or filtered water if tap water is
very hard. Work in compost to buffer soil pH gradually. If new growth stays
bleached, apply a chelated iron feed formulated for this purpose, following the
product label's rate exactly.

## Expected time to visible improvement

New leaves should emerge greener within two to four weeks once the underlying
cause is corrected. Already-bleached leaves do not regreen.

## Prognosis and when to give up

Good once the pH or water source cause is identified and corrected. Chelated iron
without addressing pH tends to give only a temporary improvement.
